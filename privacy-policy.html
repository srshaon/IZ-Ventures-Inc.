Privacy Policy for IZ Pin Scheduler

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. About this app

IZ Pin Scheduler is a personal tool that schedules and publishes pins to its owner's own Pinterest boards. It is not available to the public, and it has no users other than its owner.

2. Information the app uses

The app connects to the owner's Pinterest account through the official Pinterest API. It uses:

  • Pin content entered by the owner (images, titles, descriptions, and product links)
  • An access token issued by Pinterest after the owner authorizes the app

The app does not collect personal information about any other Pinterest user or visitor.

3. How information is stored

Pin content and the access token are stored privately in the owner's Google account (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script). They are not stored on any other server or service.

4. Sharing of information

Information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Pinterest, through its API, to create the owner's pins.

5. Access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in their Pinterest account settings. The owner can also delete all stored data by removing the Google Sheet and Apps Script project.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

7. Contact
